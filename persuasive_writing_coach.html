🎯 What is Persuasive Writing? 什么是说服性写作？（指南第1-4页）

Persuasive writing(说服性写作) tries to convince(说服) someone to agree with your opinion(观点/看法) or take action(采取行动)! You are not just sharing plain facts — you are trying to change minds and inspire(激励/鼓舞) others to help the community(社区) and world around them.

💡 一句话记住： 普通句子只是“讲事实”，而说服性写作 (Persuasive Writing) 是用坚定的语气 + 打动人的情感词 + 充足的理由，让读者产生共鸣并立刻行动！（点击下方任意带绿色虚线的难词胶囊可听发音 🔊）

👀 See the Difference: Plain Fact vs. Persuasive Writing! 一眼看懂：普通句子 vs. 说服性句子

😐 Plain Fact (普通陈述句 — 只讲事实，不够打动人)

"People cut down many trees every day. Animals live in the forest. Cutting trees is bad for the air."

（人们每天砍很多树。动物住在森林里。砍树对空气不好。—— 词汇简单，读者看完没有紧迫感。）

🔥 Persuasive Writing (高分说服句 — 点击可听朗读 🔊)

"Forests are the lungs(肺) of our planet — yet they are disappearing(消失) fast! This cruel(残忍的) destruction(破坏) harms(伤害) innocent(无辜的) animals and affects(影响) the air we all breathe(呼吸). Shouldn’t we act now to protect them?"

（用了比喻 lungs of our planet + 情感词 cruel/innocent + 反问句 Shouldn't we...，立刻让读者想要保护森林！）

📢 5 Types of Persuasive Texts & Examples 五种常见的说服性文体及例子（指南第1页）

🎤 1. Speeches (演讲稿)

To inspire(鼓舞) an audience(听众) to care or act.

Example: "Fellow(各位/同伴) students, we must stop wasting paper in our classrooms today!"

在全校集会呼吁同学们节约用纸。

📰 2. Opinion Pieces (观点文章)

To share your view about an issue(议题/问题).

Example: "Why Every City Should Ban(禁止) Single-Use(一次性的) Plastic Bags Immediately(立刻)."

在报纸/校刊发表为什么应该禁塑。

✉️ 3. Letters / Emails (倡议信)

To persuade(说服) a person or group.

Example: "Dear Principal(校长), our cafeteria(食堂) needs to replace(替换) plastic straws(吸管) with paper ones."

给校长写信建议食堂停用塑料吸管。

🪧 4. Campaign Posters (宣传海报)

To promote(宣传/推动) a cause(公益事业/目标).

Example: "Stop the waste(浪费/垃圾). Save the sea. Save our future!"

用简短有力的三连排比做环保海报。

⭐ 5. Reviews (评论/推荐)

To recommend(推荐) or warn(警告) others.

Example: "You must watch this inspiring(鼓舞人心的) nature documentary(纪录片) — it will change how you see oceans!"

强烈推荐一部环保纪录片或生态友好产品。

🍔 The Complete Essay Structure with Real Examples 文章五步结构 + 范文实例与难词翻译（指南第1、3、4页）

Every great persuasive text follows this clear step-by-step recipe. Click any box to hear the example sentence read aloud! 🔊

📌 Step 1: Title (标题 — 吸引眼球 + 亮明态度)

What to do: Use a strong action verb so readers immediately know your topic and opinion(观点).

✅ Example 1: "Protect Our Planet’s(星球的/地球的) Forests" (保护我们星球的森林)
✅ Example 2: "Say No to Harmful(有害的) Plastic Straws(吸管)!" (向有害的塑料吸管说不！)

🪝 Step 2: Introduction = Hook + Shocking Fact + Rhetorical Question (开头段三件套)

What to do: Introduce the problem, share a surprising fact, and end with a rhetorical question(反问句) that makes the reader care.

✅ Example (from Page 3):
• Hook (吸引人的比喻): "Forests are the lungs(肺) of our planet — yet they are disappearing(消失) fast."
• Fact (震撼的事实): "Every minute, we lose an area(面积/区域) of forest the size of twenty football fields(足球场)."
• Rhetorical Question (反问句收尾): "Shouldn’t we do something about it? (难道我们不该做点什么吗？)"

💡 写作困难的同学只要记住：开头写 1句问题 + 1句危害/事实 + 1句反问句 就稳拿高分！

🏛️ Step 3: Body Paragraph 1 — Big Reason & What Leaders Must Do (理由段1：政府与企业该怎么做)

What to do: Give your first strong reason and use High Modality words (must, should, Clearly) to explain what governments(政府) and companies(公司/企业) must do.

✅ Example (from Page 3): "We must act now. Governments(政府) should create stronger laws(更严格的法律) to stop illegal logging(非法砍伐树木), and companies(企业/公司) must use natural resources(自然资源) responsibly(负责任地). Clearly (显然), we cannot keep destroying(摧毁) what keeps us alive(活着)."

💡 第一段理由专门写“大机构”：记住黄金搭配 Governments must create stronger laws...（政府必须制定更严格的法律……）！

🙋 Step 4: Body Paragraph 2 — Everyday Actions for Each of Us (理由段2：我们每个人能做什么)

What to do: Involve the reader personally ("Each of us...") and list 3 concrete actions they can follow.

✅ Example (from Page 3): "Each of us can make a difference(带来改变/发挥作用). By planting trees (种树), reducing(减少) paper waste (减少纸张浪费), and supporting(支持) eco-friendly brands(环保品牌), we can protect the forests that so many living things(生物) depend on(依赖/依靠)."

💡 第二段理由从“我们每个人”写：用 By doing A, B, and C, we can... 列出3个简单小行动！

🚀 Step 5: Conclusion — Final Thought & Call to Action (结尾段：重申观点 + 号召行动)

What to do: Leave the reader feeling hopeful and responsible(有责任感的) for change with a memorable final sentence.

✅ Example 1 (Page 3): "Together, we can make a future where nature and people thrive(共同繁荣/茁壮成长)."
✅ Example 2 (Rule of 3): "Stop the waste. Save the sea. Protect our planet today!"

💡 万能结尾金句：Together, we can make a difference!（携手努力，我们定能有所作为！）

⚡ Level 1: Modality Words — Sound Confident! 第一关：情态词（让语气变坚定）

When you want to persuade someone, don't sound unsure! Use High Modality words so your reader knows it is urgent.

💡 写作秘诀： 说服别人时，不要说“也许(might/maybe)”，要用高强度情态词 (High Modality)，如 must (必须)、will (将会)、have to (不得不)、definitely (肯定地)！

🔥 High Modality (Strong & Sure - Best!)

最坚定有力，写作首选！

must 必须 will 将会 have to 必须 need to 需要 definitely 肯定地 certainly 当然 clearly 明显地 always 总是 never 绝不

🌤️ Medium Modality (Helpful for Advice)

中等语气，适合提建议

should 应该 probably 很可能 likely 可能的 often 经常 usually 通常 would 会

💧 Low Modality (Too Weak - Avoid!)

太弱、不自信，说服文尽量少用

might 也许 maybe 可能吧 perhaps 或许 could 也许能 may 可能

❤️ Level 2: Emotive Vocabulary — Touch the Reader's Heart! 第二关：情感词汇（打动读者的心）

Plain words like "big, good, bad" are boring. Emotive words make the reader feel sadness, anger, hope, or urgency!

💡 对比一下：
❌ 平淡句："Plastic pollution affects sea turtles." (塑料污染影响海龟。)
✅ 高分说服句："Plastic pollution kills thousands of innocent sea turtles each year — a heartbreaking consequence of our careless habits."

❓ Level 3A: Rhetorical Questions — Make Readers Think! 第三关(上)：反问句（不用回答，但直击人心）

A rhetorical question doesn’t need an answer — we ask it because we want people to feel the answer!

💡 反问句万能公式（指南第9页）：
把普通陈述句套进这 4 个开头，立刻变成高分反问句：
• Do we really need... ? (我们真的需要……吗？)
• If we don’t..., who will? (如果我们不……，谁会呢？)
• How can we stand by and watch...? (我们怎么能袖手旁观看着……？)
• Isn’t it time we all...? (难道不是我们所有人该……的时候了吗？)

🔁 Level 3B: Repetition & The Rule of Three 第三关(下)：重复的力量与“三连排比”（指南第10-11页）

Repeating key words or using three short, powerful phrases (Rule of Three) builds rhythm and makes your message impossible to forget!

💡 指南第10-11页核心技巧：
• Rule of Three (三连短句)： "Reduce. Reuse. Recycle." / "Stop the waste. Save the sea. Save our future."
• Phrase Repetition (开头重复)： 连续三句用 "We must protect..." 开头，气势瞬间拉满！
1️⃣ Word Repetition (单词重复)

"Protect, protect, protect our planet."

重复同一个核心词，强调重点。

2️⃣ Phrase Repetition (短语重复)

"We must act now. We must act together."

重复相同的句首短语，增强节奏。

3️⃣ Sentence Repetition (整句重复)

"We can make a difference. We can make a difference."

整句重复，加深情感共鸣。

4️⃣ The Rule of Three (三连排比)

"Reduce. Reuse. Recycle."

三个短促有力的动作，朗朗上口！

🔬 Model Essay X-Ray: "Protect Our Planet’s Forests" 第五关(上)：满分范文透视镜（指南第3-4页）

Protect Our Planet’s Forests (我们星球的森林)

[Introduction 开头: Hook + Fact + Rhetorical Question]
Forests are the lungs(肺) of our planet — yet they are disappearing(消失) fast. Every minute, we lose an area(面积) of forest the size of twenty football fields. This destruction(破坏) harms(伤害) animals and affects(影响) the air we all breathe(呼吸). Shouldn’t we do something about it? (难道我们不该做点什么吗？)

[Body Reason 1 理由段1: Governments & Companies]
We must act now. Governments(政府) should create stronger laws(更严格的法律) to stop illegal logging(非法砍伐), and companies(公司/企业) must use natural resources(自然资源) responsibly(负责任地). Clearly (显然), we cannot keep destroying(摧毁) what keeps us alive(活着).

[Body Reason 2 理由段2: What Each of Us Can Do]
Each of us can make a difference(带来改变/发挥作用). By planting trees, reducing paper waste(减少纸张浪费), and supporting(支持) eco-friendly brands(环保品牌), we can protect the forests that so many living things(生物) depend on(依赖).

[Conclusion 结尾: Inspiring Call to Action]
Together, we can make a future where nature and people thrive(共同繁荣/茁壮成长).
